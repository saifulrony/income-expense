### 3. Pricing Psychology & The "Lifetime" Secret
A common mistake mobile apps make is **Subscription Only**. Millions of users actively search Reddit, Twitter, and Play Store reviews for apps with a **Lifetime Buy Option**.
- **Subscription:** $2.49/month or $24.99/year (generates steady, predictable ARR).
- **Lifetime License:** $34.99 one-off payment.
  - *Why this works:* It creates an instant spike in cashflow on day one. Users feel they "got a bargain," give 5-star ratings, and become passionate brand evangelists who tell their colleagues.

### 4. High-Yield Affiliate Partnerships (Opt-in B2B)
In the personal finance space, affiliate marketing has the highest CPM/CPA in software:
- **High-Yield Savings & Neobanks (Wise, Revolut, Chime):** $30 – $80 per approved account.
- **Micro-Investment & Index Funds:** $25 – $60 per deposit.
- **Accounting & Business Tools (Stripe, QuickBooks):** $50 – $150 per referral.
*Crucial Rule:* These are placed respectfully in an "Explore Financial Perks" tab — **never** pop-up spam or disruptive ads.
