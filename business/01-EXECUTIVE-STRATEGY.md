### 2. Why Users Love It vs. Why Competitors Lose
| Competitor Pain Point | How CashFlow Wins | User Sentiment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intrusive Interstitial Video Ads** (e.g. 1Money, Monefy free) | **Zero banner / popup ads** in the core ledger workflow. | "This app respects my time and focus." |
| **Forced Cloud & Bank Account linking** (e.g. Mint, YNAB) | **Offline-first architecture**. All data works locally on phone. | "My financial data is private and secure." |
| **Rigid Western-only payment methods** | Multi-account support (Cash, Bank, Mobile Wallets like bKash/Nagad/UPI/Revolut). | "Designed for my real daily spending." |
| **Exorbitant $14.99/month Pricing** | Fair-tier regional pricing ($1.99 - $3.49/mo, or $29 Lifetime). | "Affordable and great value for money." |

### 3. The 4 High-Value Pillars
1. **Speed & Ergonomics:** Adding an expense takes less than 3 seconds. Floating action dock, smooth bottom sheet, calculator included.
2. **Multi-Wallet Sovereignty:** Seamlessly switch between Personal, Business, Family, and Savings.
3. **Data Independence:** 1-click JSON, CSV, and professional PDF Financial Statement export. No vendor lock-in.
4. **Progressive Power:** Simple for a student tracking pocket money; powerful enough for a freelance consultant managing client cash flow.
