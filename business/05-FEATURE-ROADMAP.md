# CashFlow — Strategic Product Roadmap & High-ROI Features

---

### Phase 1: Launch & Polish (Months 1–2) — "Organic Engine"
**Goal:** Establish exceptional UX, 4.8+ Play Store rating, and high initial retention.

- [x] **Core Expense & Income Ledger:** Fast 3-tap logging, category management, account switching.
- [x] **Theme System:** 6 harmonious palettes (Volt, Forest, Ocean, Amethyst, Slate, Light).
- [x] **Offline-First & Export:** Local SQLite / IndexedDB + PDF Statement & CSV Export.
- [ ] **Play Store Listing & Web Launch:** PWA deployment + APK signing for Google Play Store.
- [ ] **Streak & Goal Badges:** Gamification ("7-day logging streak", "Under budget this week!").
- [ ] **Quick Widget (Android):** 1-tap add transaction from the phone home screen.

---

### Phase 2: Monetization & Cloud Sync (Months 3–6) — "The Conversion Engine"
**Goal:** Introduce Pro Tier, Lifetime option, and secure recurring cash flow.

- [ ] **Cloud Sync Service:** Auto-sync between Android APK and Web via Go backend with end-to-end encryption.
- [ ] **AI Smart Receipt Scanner (OCR):**
  - Camera snapshot of a paper receipt.
  - Automatically parses total, tax, merchant name, and date.
  - Generates instant conversion from free users who hate manual typing.
- [ ] **Recurring Bills & Subscription Hunter:**
  - Track Netflix, Spotify, gym memberships, rent.
  - Sends a reminder 2 days before renewal: *"Netflix renews tomorrow for $15.49"*.
- [ ] **In-App Billing (Google Play Billing + Stripe for Web):**
  - Monthly ($2.49), Yearly ($24.99), and Lifetime ($34.99).

---

### Phase 3: Scale & Network Effects (Months 6–12) — "High-Ticket Expansion"
**Goal:** Multiply Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) with B2B and viral features.

- [ ] **Couple & Shared Family Wallets:** Two phones live-syncing one shared household balance.
- [ ] **Freelancer / Sole Trader Mode ($5.99/mo):**
  - Track deductible business expenses.
  - Generate quarterly Tax & Profit/Loss reports.
  - 1-click Invoice creation to send to clients.
- [ ] **Smart Budget Alerts (Push Notifications):**
  - *"Warning: You have reached 80% of your Dining budget with 12 days left in the month."*
- [ ] **Fintech Affiliate Hub (Opt-In):**
  - Exclusive partner discounts on high-yield savings accounts and cashback credit cards.
