### 1. Organic Viral Loops

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["New User Joins Free"] --> B["Uses for 1-2 Weeks\nTracks Daily Expenses"]
    B --> C["Taps 'Share Budget'\nfor Couple or Roommate"]
    C --> D["Partner Downloads App\nvia Custom Invite Link"]
    D --> E["2 Active Users Formed"]
    E --> F["Both Upgrade to Pro Duo\nor Refer 3 Friends"]
```

#### Loop A: The "Couple & Roommate" Invite Loop
- Personal finance is shared between couples, families, and housemates.
- By offering **Shared Household Budgets**, one user naturally brings in their spouse or roommates.
- Every active user has a K-Factor (viral coefficient) of **0.25–0.40**, driving organic acquisition without spending on paid ads.

#### Loop B: "Receipt / Expense Split" Sharing
- Users can export an aesthetic summary or split an expense bill with a clean graphic card.
- At the bottom: *"Tracked with CashFlow — Free & Offline"*.

### 3. Acquisition Channels (Zero-to-Low CAC)

1. **App Store & Play Store Optimization (ASO):**
   - High-volume keywords: *"offline expense tracker"*, *"cash in cash out"*, *"daily budget manager"*, *"no ads spending app"*.
   - Visual mockups emphasizing privacy and speed.
2. **Short-Form Content (TikTok / IG Reels / YouTube Shorts):**
   - Theme: *"How I track my finances in 5 seconds without letting big tech see my bank"* (privacy/minimalism resonates heavily with Gen Z and Millennials).
3. **Product Communities:**
   - Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit (`r/personalfinance`, `r/SideProject`, `r/androidapps`).
