### 2. Where Competitors Stumble & How CashFlow Capitalizes

1. **User Anger over Ads:** Look at Google Play Store 1-star reviews for Monefy or 1Money — over 60% of complaints are *"Too many video ads popping up while I'm typing!"*.
   - **CashFlow's Stance:** **Zero banner ads.** Clean, premium aesthetic even for free users.
2. **Subscription Fatigue:** Users are exhausted by $10–$15/month subscriptions for simple trackers.
   - **CashFlow's Stance:** An accessible **$2.49/month** or **$34.99 Lifetime** license converts casual users who would otherwise abandon paid apps.
3. **No Web App:** Most Android competitors have no web dashboard; users can't review their monthly finances on their laptop.
   - **CashFlow's Stance:** Responsive Next.js Web App works identically on Desktop, Mac, Tablet, and Mobile.
